Sonicfast 0.15.1 tidies the audio output picker and makes self-hosted Cast receivers easier to use.

- The player's output picker is now a compact list of icons and names: **This
  device**, **Snapcast** and each discovered AirPlay or Cast receiver, with a
  check on the active output. Snapcast is listed only once sending is set up.
  Unsupported receivers are dimmed and explain why on hover.
- The picker looks for receivers each time it opens; a refresh button scans again.
  With LAN casting off, a single switch enables it.
- Choosing a Cast receiver now verifies it before switching. Genuine Google Cast
  devices connect as before. A receiver that cannot prove its identity opens a
  **Verify Cast receiver** dialog where you can paste its SHA-256 certificate
  fingerprint (with or without `:` separators).
- Pasted fingerprints are checked before use and remembered per receiver, so the
  receiver keeps working after an address change. A saved fingerprint that no
  longer matches asks again instead of connecting.

Validation: Linux automated tests and the Docker AirPlay/Cast interoperability
tests passed, including the certificate-pinned software Cast target and wrong-pin
rejection. Native captures reviewed the picker and dialog. The new verify-then-
switch flow has not yet been exercised against a physical Chromecast or a
self-hosted receiver through the picker; Windows/macOS were not tested.

Install updates manually; the in-app self-updater remains disabled.
